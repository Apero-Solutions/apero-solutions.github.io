How it works

Three steps to data in the cloud.

No middleware, no database to stand up, no monthly platform fee. Wire it in and you are logging.

01

Wire it in

Connect your energy meter, PLC, VFD, or sensor over Modbus RTU (RS-485) or Modbus TCP.

02

Configure once

Enter your Wi-Fi or Ethernet details and a Google service account. Nothing else to buy or subscribe to.

03

Watch data flow

Readings land in your Sheet on the interval you choose — or publish over MQTT into a larger system.

Specification

Everything on one small box.

Inputs

Modbus RTU & TCP

Read RS-485 serial devices or Modbus TCP equipment, single or multiple slaves.

Outputs

Sheets & MQTT

Publish directly to Google Sheets, or over MQTT for a full data pipeline.

Connectivity

Wi-Fi or Ethernet

Bring the device online whichever way suits the site.

Reliability

Offline buffering

Lose connection and readings are held on the device, then pushed once you're back — no gaps in your data.

Security

Encrypted publishing

MQTT over TLS/SSL, with credentials stored securely on the device.

Alerting

No-internet output

A dedicated output flags a dropped connection, so unattended installs never fail silently.

Maintenance

Over-the-air updates

Push firmware remotely — no site visit, no downtime.

Ownership

Your own account

Data lands in a Google Sheet you own, through your own service account.

Infrastructure

None required

No server to run, no broker to host, no code to write to get started.

Note: uplink is over Wi-Fi or Ethernet. When Ethernet is used for the uplink, Modbus input is via RTU (RS-485).
