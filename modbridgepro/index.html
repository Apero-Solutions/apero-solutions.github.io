How it works

Three steps to data in the cloud.

No middleware, no database to stand up, no monthly platform fee. Wire it in and you are logging.

01

Wire it in

Connect your energy meter, PLC, VFD, or sensor over Modbus RTU or Modbus TCP.

02

Configure once

Enter your Wi-Fi or Ethernet details and a Google service account.

03

Watch data flow

Readings land in your Sheet on the interval you choose — or publish over MQTT.

Specification

Everything on one small box.

Inputs

Modbus RTU & TCP

Read RS-485 serial devices or Modbus TCP equipment.

Outputs

Sheets & MQTT

Publish directly to Google Sheets or over MQTT.

Connectivity

Wi-Fi or Ethernet

Bring the device online whichever way suits the site.

Reliability

Offline buffering

Hold readings during an outage and push them when connected.

Security

Encrypted publishing

MQTT over TLS/SSL with credentials stored securely.

Maintenance

Over-the-air updates

Push firmware remotely with no site visit.
